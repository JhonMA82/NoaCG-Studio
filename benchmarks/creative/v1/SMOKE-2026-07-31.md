# Creative Mode pilot - bracket smoke run, 2026-07-31

The first paid run of `bench:creative:pilot` (docs/CREATIVE_MODE_PLAN.md §10.3, §11): all 8
bracket briefs x 4 arms on the calibrated open candidate, run to decide ONE question - is
the rig trustworthy enough to justify full candidate passes? Raw artifacts (pilot.json per
run, hold frames, generated code, sameness.json) live in the local `creative-pilot-out/`
of the worktree that ran it (branch `claude/creative-mode-phase-c-d96bc6`); this file is
the part of the result that must survive that directory - the vision round's scorecards
were worktree-only and are gone, which is a mistake this report exists to not repeat.

- Candidate route: `openrouter:qwen/qwen3-30b-a3b-instruct-2507` ($0.05/$0.20 per M)
- Critique route (arm D): `openrouter:google/gemini-2.5-flash`
- Diagnostic re-runs: br-8team arms A/B on `openrouter:qwen/qwen3-coder-next`
- Recorded spend: **$0.12** (floor - a call that dies with `malformed_response` bills
  tokens the client never sees; the server's `ai_gateway_requests` ledger has the truth)
- Runs were split A,B / C,D per brief (shell time cap), which re-runs the shared intent
  stage per split: ~$0.0004 extra per brief, harmless to attribution.

## Verdict: the RIG is trustworthy; the DATA does not justify full candidate passes yet

The plumbing did its job everywhere it was asked: explicit routes fail closed, prices gate,
the ceiling holds, per-stage token/cost/model attribution lands (after one mid-smoke fix,
below), hold frames capture, the structural check fires with named parts, per-arm artifacts
and sameness metrics write, and the free sameness calibration behaves (catalog min-pair
0.55 = br01~br02, mean pair 3.00). What the run measured, however, is four blockers that a
full pass would only re-measure 4 models x 29 briefs more expensively:

### Blocker 1 - the candidate cannot complete the coder-shaped arms

`qwen3-30b` finished **0/8 arm-A runs and 3/8 arm-B runs**; every failure is the gateway's
`malformed_response` on a large template emit. The stage ledger shows the shape exactly
(br-8team-create A: coder emit 10.2k tokens OK -> repair-1 10.4k OK -> repair-2 dies).
Small structured emits are fine - the same model completed **8/8 arm-C** runs (concepts /
spec / bounded style patches) and its intent stage was 24/24 in the routing bench. On
`qwen3-coder-next` the same arms complete (2/3, one stochastic failure). Consequence: the
pilot's one-route-for-all-arms design conflates "arm quality" with "emit-size reliability".
A vs B vs C on one cheap model is not measurable this way; plan §4's per-stage model
classes need to reach the RIG (candidate route for coder-class arms, fast route already
separate) or arm A/B candidates must be coder-class models.

### Blocker 2 - the scaffold under-composes the bracket family (§3.3's named risk, observed)

Arm C completed 8/8 and went 5/8 "valid" - and the frames show the scaffold's floor is a
single flat row (or column) of team names, not a tree. The style stage cannot add
structure by design (CSS + bounded slot HTML), so no bracket from arms C/D reads as a
bracket. This is exactly the risk §3.3 wrote down ("every CREATE result becomes a reskin
of the scaffold's one skeleton") landing on the first relational category. The sanctioned
rival (a) - the bounded layout IR compiling rows/columns/stacks/split - is what this
evidence points at; phase E's condition ("only if the diversity criteria say the scaffold
under-composes") is met on brackets before diversity is even measurable.

### Blocker 3 - declared states never reach the result (FIXED in this change)

The dominant structural gap (12 of 15 completed C/D results, plus both completed coder
results): the intent declares operator states (`winner-reveal`, `lock-in`,
`highlight-current-round`, ...) and the assembled result has no machine and no middle
step. Localized exactly: the stage-5 specs DID declare the states, but the spec schema
does not force `revealRegions`, cheap models routinely omit it, and
`scaffold.ts buildAnimData` skipped any state whose reveals resolved to nothing - a
declared operator beat silently deleted at compile time. Fixed the clamp-don't-fail way
(plan §3.4): such a state now compiles to a degraded-but-present emphasis step the author
refines in the timeline; pinned free in e2e/creative-pilot.spec.ts with the
reveals-present twin.

### Blocker 4 - the critique repair never lands (0/7)

`styleRepairFromCritique` only returns a repair when the repaired template passes FULL
validation, so on an already-invalid base (4 of 7 D runs) it structurally cannot land, and
on the 3 valid bases the focused patch failed validation or the gate anyway. Arm D
currently prices a critique that cannot act on its findings; §11 criterion 8's
improves/damages rates cannot be measured until a repair can land at all.

### What the frames look like (the would-be human-review material)

No sampled frame is reviewable as a broadcast graphic: flat rows of names, top-left
unstyled debug-style lists (one printing `crest-placeholder.png` as literal text), opaque
full-frame black backgrounds on two "valid" results (an overlay must not paint the frame),
and several empty captures whose rows carry `bench-entrance` findings (the template really
never brings content on - not a capture race). A pairwise "which would you air?" over
these would rank garbage against garbage; running it would spend reviewer trust, not earn
evidence. Engineering validity says 5/8 of arm C passed; the pictures say none of it is
airworthy - the F5 inversion, live, which is why the two scorecards stay separate.

## §11 criteria - what the smoke can and cannot say

| criterion | smoke reading |
|---|---|

| 2 structural satisfaction | C/D: 3/15 gap-free ≈ 20%, vs the 90% bar - dominated by dropped states (blocker 3) |
| 3 engineering validity | reported, never ranked: A n/a (0 completions), B 1/3, C 5/8, D 3/7 |
| 4 human preference | NOT RUNNABLE - no reviewable material (above) |
| 5 concept diversity | mixed (5/8 briefs with >=2 distinct directions); real signal needs the full bank |

| 7 cost/latency | C p50 ~$0.0013 / 48-240s; D ~$0.004 / 82-247s; A/B unmeasurable on this candidate |
| 8 critique rates | NOT MEASURABLE - repairs cannot land (blocker 4) |

## Before a full pass is worth paying for

1. Per-arm model routing in the rig (or coder-class candidates for A/B) - blocker 1.
2. ~~States carried through the scaffold compile~~ - blocker 3, fixed and pinned in this
   change (verify on the next paid run: the states gap rate should collapse).
3. A scaffold (or bounded layout IR) that can compose a tree before the bracket category
   is re-run - blocker 2; versus/lower-third may be runnable sooner since their structures
   are single-panel. This is an OWNER call: it is the §3.3 rival (a), i.e. phase-E scope
   pulled forward on evidence.
4. A decision on the critique-repair acceptance rule - blocker 4 (bench-only code).
5. The black-stage frames: checked statically - the templates do NOT paint body/html (the
   scaffold keeps them transparent and no override touches them), so the opaque black is a
   capture/compose interaction (suspect list: the color-scheme iframe gotcha, or the stage
   gradient's near-black corners reading as black at a glance). Needs one live repro.
   Separately CONFIRMED: no gate anywhere measures background opacity
   (validateTemplate + runtimeBench grep clean) - if CREATE results can paint the frame,
   that dimension is unmeasured; the clip-path lesson applies (measure it or forbid it).
6. Discovered in the artifacts, not yet fixed: a spec with TWO repeating regions compiles
   BOTH as `<div class="creative-rows" id="creative-rows">` - duplicate DOM ids, so the
   list runtime feeds only the first and the second stays empty (seen in
   br-in-progress C). Either one repeating region per scaffold (the house one-textarea
   convention suggests exactly one) or per-region ids with a runtime that rebuilds each.

Mid-smoke rig fix (committed): the control arm now keeps its telemetry-ring stage ledger
on failure, so a failed run attributes its spend and names the stage that died.
