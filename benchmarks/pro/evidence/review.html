NoaCG Pro - the evidence page

Every frame here was captured at 1920x1080 with its alpha intact and is composited over the backdrop chosen above. The backdrops are drawn by this page in CSS - they stand in for footage, they are not footage, so read them for contrast rather than as a real shot. Nothing on this page carries a verdict: the measurement beside each picture is raw, and what it means is yours to say.

The marked straps

24 mark-capable lower thirds, each rendered bare and then carrying badge-square and shield-tall. A square and a portrait mark fail differently and the sweep only ever rendered the square one, which is why two of these read clean for so long.
