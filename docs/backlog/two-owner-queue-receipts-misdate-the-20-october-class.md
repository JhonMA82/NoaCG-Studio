# Two owner-queue receipts misdate the class as 20 October

**Filed:** 2026-09-16, during the 2026-09-16 handoff drain (row TE), carried over from
`docs/handoffs/2026-09-16-sk-what-october-needs.md`, which found this and explicitly declined to
edit the receipts itself - they are dated records of a landing, not its author's to rewrite.

## Why

`docs/acceptance/owner-queue/2026-09-15-combined-controls-where-the-show-is-run.md` calls the
hosted control page "the surface you and a class will be holding on 2026-10-20", and
`docs/acceptance/owner-queue/2026-09-16-a-profile-driven-where-the-show-is-run.md` repeats it and
calls the day "the night". Per ALIGN-2026-09-15-6 (recorded in `docs/OWNER_RULINGS.md`), there is
no class on 20 October - that is the 25 September lecture. The hosted-control-page claim itself
still holds; only the date and the "class"/"night" framing are wrong.

## What it would take

A decision by whoever owns the queue on whether a landed, dated receipt gets corrected in place or
left as a record of what was believed at the time, with a note added instead. Not a mechanical
fix - editing a dated receipt's own claim is a judgement call the row that found this declined to
make on its own.

`docs/handoffs/2026-09-16-sk-what-october-needs.md`, "Traps that exist in no repo file": "Two
owner-queue receipts now read wrong about this date and I did not edit them, because they are
